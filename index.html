Slow-Space Scaling

MuonW, 128K → 2M (k = 16), 1.70B tokens. Keep the fast directions of training (the top-r of the preconditioned Gauss-Newton) at the 128K batch and scale only their complement to 2M. Does that remove the large-batch penalty that no whole-model scaling rule removes? Updated ; the page reloads itself every 5 minutes.

What the names mean

Reference 128K
The 128K run continued unchanged (k = 1). Every number below is a penalty against it, in millinats (mnat = 0.001 nats/token) of the 10M-token validation loss at step 13,000.
Baseline: all at 2M
Whole-model batch scaling: every parameter takes one update per 2M tokens. Matrices follow the recipe; the auxiliary AdamW (embeddings, head, scalars) uses the sweep's best transport (LR fixed, β₁ fixed, β₂ → β₂¹⁶, WD fixed). This is the paper's setting; from init it reproduces the production sweep (+155 / +122 / +199 vs the sweep's +158 / +115 / +195).
Baseline: matrices at 2M
Only the 72 hidden matrices at 2M; the auxiliary parameters stay at 128K. The gap to "all at 2M" is the auxiliary block's own penalty.
Control: random r kept
Negative control for the slow-only arms: a fixed random r-dimensional subspace of the matrices is kept at 128K instead of the top-r. It should, and does, equal the matrices-at-2M baseline.
Slow-only r tracked
The headline intervention. Top-r eigenspace of P^{1/2} G P^{1/2} (Gauss-Newton G, Muon's polar preconditioner P) kept at 128K; its complement at 2M under the recipe; aux at 128K. Tracked: the eigenproblem is re-solved every 64 base steps (one warm block iteration, 272 HVPs) and the parameter-space projector orth(P_t^{1/2}U) is re-mapped through the live preconditioner every 16 steps.
Slow-only r frozen
Same split, but the top-r is computed once at the anchor and never updated. Tells how much the tracking matters.
Slow-only + aux at 2M
Ablation: top-r kept, the complement and the auxiliary parameters at 2M. Closes the decomposition penalty ≈ aux + fast matrix directions + slow directions.
Recipes
cvx √κ·η, μ, λ (our bound-minimization rule) · llm η, μ, λ→κλ (the sweep's best rule) · pl1 κ·η, μ, λ (Power Lines p = 1) · sde κ·η, μ→μ^κ, λ (dropped after its first endpoints). Applied to the scaled channel only; λ is the coupled decay coefficient in W ← (1 − ηλ)W − ηU.
Families
from init (step 8): branched after 8 base steps (Muon's preconditioner needs a momentum), the paper-comparable setting. anchor 1000: branched at step 1,000 of the 128K run, i.e. the first 7.7% of tokens are taken at 128K; a cheaper diagnostic that shows how much of the penalty lives in early training.

Bars and cells are penalties against each family's own Reference 128K. Running arms show their step; queued arms a dash. The slow-only rows use r = 256 (0.3% of the 84.9M matrix dimensions, about 4% of the preconditioned Gauss-Newton trace).

Rank sweep · how many directions must be kept (from init)

Penalty against Reference 128K as a function of the kept rank r, tracked (solid) and frozen (dashed). Tracking a 768-wide basis is not affordable, so r = 768 is frozen only.

Auxiliary block · where the whole-model penalty sits (from init)

Decomposition of the all-at-2M penalty: the auxiliary block (all at 2M − matrices at 2M), the fast matrix directions (matrices at 2M − slow-only tracked r256) and the slow directions (slow-only tracked r256 itself). The aux-at-2M ablation tests whether these parts add.

Early training · anchor 1000 vs from init

The same arms in both families. The difference is the first 1,000 base steps (131M tokens) taken at 128K in the anchor-1000 family; it also flips the recipe order (√κ·η is destructive only from init).
